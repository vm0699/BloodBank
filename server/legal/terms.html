What LifeLink is

LifeLink is a free platform that helps people find voluntary blood donors, blood banks, donation camps and organ-donation information. We connect people; we are not a hospital, blood bank or medical provider.

Using LifeLink

  • You must be 18 or older and provide true information about yourself.
  • One person, one account. Do not use someone else's identity or documents.
  • Post a blood request only for a real patient. Never post false, repeated or test requests.
  • Donating is voluntary and unpaid. Buying or selling blood or organs is illegal. Do not ask for or offer money; report anyone who does.
  • Use contact details you receive only to arrange the donation. Do not store, share or misuse them.
  • Do not harass other users, scrape the app, or try to bypass its security.

Medical responsibility

Whether you may donate, and whether blood is safe to use, is always decided by a qualified medical officer at the collection site. Eligibility and compatibility shown in the app are guidance only. Do not rely on LifeLink for emergency care - call your local emergency number or go to the nearest hospital.

Verification

Donors are asked to verify their identity through DigiLocker. A verified badge means the identity was confirmed from official documents; it is not a medical clearance.

Organ pledge

A pledge recorded in LifeLink states your wish and informs your family. It does not replace the legal consent process. Please also register on the national portal and tell your family.

Suspension and removal

We may suspend or remove accounts or requests that break these terms, are reported as fraudulent, or put others at risk. You can delete your account at any time from Settings.
