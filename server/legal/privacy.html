Privacy Policy

LifeLink, operated by SAVRO · Last updated 14 October 2026

LifeLink connects people who need blood with verified donors nearby, lists blood banks and donation camps, and lets you record an organ-donation pledge. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

DataWhy
Mobile numberTo sign you in with a one-time code and to let a requester and an accepting donor reach each other.
Name, date of birth, gender, blood group, weight, heightTo decide who you can help and whether you are eligible to donate.
City, area, pincode, approximate locationTo show you nearby requests and, if you switch on availability, to show other users that a donor of your blood group is nearby.
Health screening answersTo pause your donor listing when a short-term condition applies. They are never shown to other users.
Identity verification (DigiLocker)To confirm that donors are real people. We receive your name, date of birth, gender, the last 4 digits of your Aadhaar and the names of the documents you choose to share. We never store your full Aadhaar number or a copy of your Aadhaar. We never see your DigiLocker password or code.
Donation history, requests, offersTo run the service, keep your donation gap and show your impact.
Organ pledge, family contact and witness detailsOnly if you choose to pledge. Shown to authorised hospital coordinators, not to other app users.
Emergency contactKept on your profile for your safety. Not shown to other users.
Device notification tokenTo send alerts when the app is closed.

Who can see what

  • Other users see only your first name and initial, blood group, approximate position (always shifted by a few hundred metres, never exact), how recently you were active, and your verified badge.
  • Your phone number is shared only with the person whose request you agreed to help, and only after they accept your offer - and theirs with you.
  • LifeLink administrators and blood-bank staff can see profiles to review verification and run camps; every access is logged.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Sharing with service providers

We use providers to run the service: an identity-verification partner for DigiLocker, an SMS provider to deliver sign-in codes, a cloud database host, a push-notification service, and OpenStreetMap for map tiles. They process data only to provide those services.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete my account; this removes your profile, notifications, donation records and any stored documents. Requests other people still see are anonymised. Verification consent from DigiLocker is time-limited and expires on its own.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your details in Profile → Personal & medical details.
  • Download a copy of your data from Settings → Download my data.
  • Switch off availability at any time to disappear from the map.
  • Withdraw your organ pledge at any time.
  • Delete your account and data from Settings. You can also write to us {{SUPPORT}}.

Security

Connections are encrypted. Sign-in codes are checked by our SMS provider and never stored in readable form. Sessions can be revoked, and staff access is limited by role and logged. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

LifeLink is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Medical disclaimer

LifeLink is not a medical service. Eligibility shown in the app is guidance based on common norms; a medical officer always makes the final decision. Blood and organs must never be bought or sold.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy in a way that matters we will tell you in the app. Questions or complaints: write to us {{SUPPORT}}.
